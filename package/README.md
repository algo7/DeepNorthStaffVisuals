# DeepNorthStaffVisuals

End-game staffs shouldn't look plain. Give Echo Spike and Lightning Strike the game's own visual effects.

Available effects:

- Frostfire flames
- Thunderblood lightning
- Bleeding (Ashlands)
- Storming (Ashlands)
- Primal (Ashlands)
- Dyrnwyn's fire
- Frostner's frost

> **Visuals only.** The mod only changes how the staff looks. No extra damage or effects, the staff works just like
> vanilla.

![Seven Lightning Strikes on item stands along a wall, each wearing a different look](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/algo7/DeepNorthStaffVisuals/main/images/lightning-strike-looks.jpg)

## How to Use

- Equip a staff and press **L** to change its look. Each press shows the next one, then back to plain.
- Works on Echo Spike and Lightning Strike only.
- Each staff keeps its look: in chests, on item stands and armor stands, and on the ground.
- Upgrading gives you a new, plain staff. Press **L** to pick its look again.

![A player at night holding Lightning Strike in two looks: on fire, and glowing blue](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/algo7/DeepNorthStaffVisuals/main/images/in-hand.jpg)

## Multiplayer

Client-side only. Friends who also have the mod see your looks, and you see theirs. Friends without it see plain staffs.

## Compatibility

- Built and tested for Valheim 1.0 (Deep North), on Linux, with no OS-specific code.
- It only adds effects to those two staffs, so it shouldn't clash with other mods, except ones that also change how they
  look.
- **L** taken by another mod? Change the key in the mod's config file (`BepInEx/config/algo7.deepnorthstaffvisuals.cfg`).

## Uninstalling

Just remove it. Your staffs go back to plain. Each staff's look stays saved on it (the game ignores it), so
reinstalling brings it back.
