# WeaponAuras

**Test build.** Give your plain weapons the game's own effects: Frostfire flames, Thunderblood lightning, the Ashlands
Bleeding, Storming and Primal effects, Dyrnwyn's fire or Frostner's frost.

## How to Use

- Equip a weapon and press **L** to change its look. Each press shows the next one, then back to plain.
- Works on swords, axes, maces, knives, spears, atgeirs, bows, crossbows and staffs that have no effect of their own.
- The look starts plain every time you enter a world.

## Multiplayer

Client-side only. Only you see the looks.

Made with AI assistance.
