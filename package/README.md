# WeaponAuras

**Test build.** Deep North staffs deserve to look the part. Give Echo Spike and Lightning Strike the game's own weapon
effects:
Frostfire flames, Thunderblood lightning, the Ashlands Bleeding, Storming and Primal effects, Dyrnwyn's fire or
Frostner's frost.

## How to Use

- Equip a staff and press **L** to change its look. Each press shows the next one, then back to plain.
- Works on Echo Spike and Lightning Strike.
- The look starts plain every time you enter a world.

## Multiplayer

Client-side only. Only you see the looks.

Made with AI assistance.
