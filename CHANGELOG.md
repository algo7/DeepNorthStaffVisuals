# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- L changes the look of an equipped Echo Spike or Lightning Strike (Frostfire, Thunderblood, Bleeding,
  Storming, Primal, Dyrnwyn, Frostner, or plain).
- Friends who also have the mod see your look, and you see theirs.
- Each staff keeps its own look, also on item stands, armor stands and the ground, with a "Look" line in its tooltip.
