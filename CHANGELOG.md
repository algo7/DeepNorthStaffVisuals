# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Thunderblood is calmer and no longer leans to one side: fewer, shorter bolts on both sides of the staff alike, and its
  sparks come off all around it.

## 1.0.0

- First release: press L to give an equipped Echo Spike or Lightning Strike one of the game's own visual effects
  (Frostfire, Thunderblood, Bleeding, Storming, Primal, Dyrnwyn, Frostner), or plain.
- Each staff keeps its own look, also on item stands, armor stands and the ground, with a "Look" line in its tooltip.
- Friends who also have the mod see your looks, and you see theirs.
